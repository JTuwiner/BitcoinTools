  <p>NOWPayments is the payment-gateway product from the ChangeNOW team. Your customer pays in whatever coin they have; you take it as that coin or convert it to something you actually want to hold. They cover 350+ assets, plus plugins, payment links, and a widget if you don't want to write API code.</p>

  <p>The fee is <strong>0.5%</strong> when the customer pays in the same asset you settle in, and <strong>1%</strong> when there's a conversion. Payouts to your wallet have no service fee, just network fees. You get an API key from the dashboard after you sign up at <a href="https://nowpayments.io" target="_blank" rel="noopener">nowpayments.io</a>. Typical flow: create a payment with <code>POST https://api.nowpayments.io/v1/payment</code> and an <code>x-api-key</code> header, then listen for IPN callbacks.</p>

  <p>One thing to know: new accounts are <strong>custodial by default</strong>. Funds land in a NOWPayments balance until you withdraw. You can add a payout wallet and turn custody off if you want coins to go straight out. Don't treat it as BTCPay. And don't mix it up with ChangeNOW — that's a <a href="/tools/best-crypto-apis/#changenow">swap API</a>, not checkout.</p>

API and WooCommerce pulls are 1% with a $15 cap. Sign up at <a href="https://app.zaprite.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener">app.zaprite.com</a>.</p>

<h2 id="types">Types of Crypto Payment Gateways</h2>

<p>Most roundups treat these like they're interchangeable. They're not:</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Multi-coin hosted checkout</strong> — NOWPayments. Many assets, API, plugins, optional conversion.</li>
  <li><strong>Bitcoin-only, keep the coins</strong> — Zaprite. Invoices and links to your wallet. No altcoins.</li>
  <li><strong>Self-hosted Lightning</strong> — BTCPay Server. $0 processor fee, you run it.</li>
  <li><strong>Bitcoin in, fiat out</strong> — OpenNode. Convert some or all of each payment to a bank.</li>
  <li><strong>Stablecoins on a card processor</strong> — Stripe. USDC, not bitcoin.</li>
  <li><strong>POS / invoices / payroll brand</strong> — BitPay. Custodial, KYC, higher fees.</li>
  <li><strong>Swap APIs</strong> — ChangeNOW. That's <a href="/tools/best-crypto-apis/">a different article</a>. Not merchant checkout.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="choose">How to Choose a Crypto Payment Gateway</h2>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Custody.</strong> BTCPay and Zaprite keep keys out of the processor. NOWPayments is custodial by default (you can switch). OpenNode, Stripe, and BitPay hold funds until payout.</li>
  <li><strong>Bitcoin vs everything else.</strong> Zaprite, BTCPay, and OpenNode are bitcoin (and Lightning). NOWPayments is hundreds of coins. Stripe is stablecoins only.</li>
  <li><strong>Do you need dollars?</strong> OpenNode or BitPay. BTCPay will not wire you USD.</li>
  <li><strong>Lightning.</strong> BTCPay if you'll run channels. OpenNode or Zaprite if you want Lightning without the node ops.</li>
  <li><strong>Already on Stripe?</strong> Turn on stablecoins. Don't bolt on a second bitcoin processor unless you actually need BTC.</li>
</ul>

<p>The three mistakes I see most often: using Stripe when you meant bitcoin, using BTCPay when you needed a bank payout, and using ChangeNOW as a checkout page.</p>
