Coldcard Security Advisory

Coinkite disclosed a vulnerability affecting seed phrase generation on Coldcard devices. Private keys generated on Mk3, Mk4, Mk5, and Q models may have fewer random bits than expected, making them potentially vulnerable to attack. Moving a Coldcard-generated seed to a Trezor, Ledger, or any other device does not fix it — new keys must be generated on non-Coldcard hardware.

This comparison is for product specs only. We are not recommending new Coldcard purchases while the advisory is active.

Why single-sig is risky: If you hold bitcoin with one key on one vendor’s device, a single bug or disclosure can put your funds at immediate risk. That is the lesson of this incident for anyone using a lone hardware wallet.

Why multivendor multisig matters: In a 2-of-3 setup with keys from different manufacturers (plus a collaborative partner), one compromised device does not by itself let an attacker move your bitcoin. Unchained vaults are built around that model — you hold two keys, Unchained holds one, and their key was not generated on Coldcard.
